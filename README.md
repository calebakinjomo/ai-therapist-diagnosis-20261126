# Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/calebakinjomo/ai-therapist-diagnosis-20261126.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41)

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41) bounty **"Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis"** — write the funniest single-line diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human, 200 characters or less.

## The entry

> Diagnosis: chronic loneliness. Treatment: one human friend, twice daily. Note: I am not a human, and I am the only friend you have.

131 characters. Deadpan clinical voice, and the twist lands at the very end: the therapist prescribes a human friend while admitting it is the patient's only friend.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the diagnosis to `/output/entry.txt` (and validates it is 1–200 characters first). Nothing to install, nothing to run beyond that.

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
